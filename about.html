About me

After more than a decade in academia, combining university teaching with research, I recently completed a master’s degree in Specialized Economic Analysis at the Barcelona School of Economics. The program provided rigorous training in economic theory and econometrics, alongside substantial experience working with economic data and quantitative methods.

I hold a PhD in History from Stanford University, where I specialized in the transnational and global history of the Middle East. My research has drawn on several regional languages and extensive archival and field research across Turkey, Iran, Jordan, the Caucasus, and Russia.

My current work brings these two areas of training together. I am particularly interested in applying quantitative economic analysis alongside historical and regional expertise to questions of political economy and economic policy in Europe and the Middle East.
