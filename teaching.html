Courses Taught

Italy and the Islamic Mediterranean

Dickinson College, Bologna

Examines Italy's historical relationships with Muslim societies, from medieval Sicily and the Renaissance to colonialism, the Cold War, and contemporary immigration politics. Explores how these encounters shaped Italian identity and challenged conventional divisions between Europe and the Islamic world.

Migrants, Radicals, and Dictators: Italy and the Middle East in the Modern Era

Stanford University in Florence

Explores Italy's political, economic, and cultural relationships with the Middle East from the nineteenth century to the present. Topics include Italian nationalism, colonialism, fascism, Cold War alliances, migration, and contemporary geopolitics.

[Syllabus] [Primary Sources]

World History: 3500 BCE–1300 CE

University of Maryland, College Park

A global survey of human societies from the emergence of the earliest states through the medieval period. Emphasizes comparative history, political and economic institutions, religion, migration, and the development of long-distance networks connecting different world regions.
